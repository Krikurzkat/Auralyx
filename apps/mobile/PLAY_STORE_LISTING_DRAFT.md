# Google Play listing draft

## App name

Auralyx

## Short description

Play and search the music saved on your Android device.

## Full description

Auralyx is a focused music player for the audio saved on your Android device. Browse your local library, search for tracks, and keep listening with background playback controls.

- Scan and browse on-device audio after you grant Music and audio permission.
- Search your local library by track name.
- Play tracks with Android playback controls available while Auralyx is in the background.
- No account is required.

Audio files and track details are used on your device for browsing and playback; Auralyx does not upload your music library to its servers.
