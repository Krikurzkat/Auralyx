# Auralyx Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 21, 2026**

This policy applies to the Auralyx Android app.

## Audio library access

If you grant Music and audio permission, Auralyx reads audio files and basic track details, such as file name and duration, so it can display and play music saved on your device. You can deny or revoke this permission in Android settings.

## How information is used

The Android app uses your local audio library for browsing and playback. Audio files and their details stay on your device and are not uploaded to Auralyx servers. Track details may be shown by Android in its system playback controls while a track is playing.

The current Android version does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics code. Auralyx does not sell or share your audio files or track details.

## Storage and deletion

Auralyx does not keep a server-side copy of your music library. Revoke Music and audio permission at any time in Android settings. Uninstalling Auralyx removes app-held data; it does not delete your original music files.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on Auralyx’s Google Play listing.

This policy applies to Auralyx for Android, version 1.0.0 and later.
